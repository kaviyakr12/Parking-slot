Smart Parking Slot Allocation System

To efficiently manage parking spaces and reduce congestion by automatically tracking slot availability, booking status, and vehicle checkout, ensuring optimal utilization of parking areas.

How it works:
The system allows users to select a city and area, view available parking slots, and book a slot for a specific duration. Slots are color-coded for clarity:

Green: Available

Yellow: Booked and within the reserved time

Red: Time exceeded or expired (with hover effects to highlight urgency)

The system calculates the cost based on the actual duration parked and allows users to take their vehicle before the end time by paying for the minutes or hours used. Real-time updates are reflected in the interface, and email notifications are sent for booking confirmation, reminders, and checkout.

Tech Stack:

Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript

Responsive Design: Ensures compatibility across devices
